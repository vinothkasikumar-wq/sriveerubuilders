04 How we build

Five stages, no black boxes

Pre-construction is where projects are won or lost. We front-load the planning so the field crew builds from a clean set — not a running list of guesses.

01
Week 1–2

Consult & Site Survey

We walk the site, take measurements, pull the plat and talk budget honestly before a dollar is committed.

02
Week 3–5

Design & Fixed Estimate

Drawings, material selections and a line-item bid you can actually read — locked as a fixed price.

03
Week 5–8

Permits & Scheduling

We handle the permit package, inspections calendar and a look-ahead schedule down to the trade day.

04
Build phase

Construction

Self-perform crews, weekly owner walk-throughs and a clean, hoarded, hazard-marked site every single day.

05
Closeout

Inspection & Handover

Final inspections, punch list, warranty binder, as-builts and an 11-month follow-up walkthrough.
